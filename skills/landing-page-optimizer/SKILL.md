# Landing Page Conversion Optimizer

## Purpose

Diagnose and improve landing-page relevance, trust and conversion flow.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the paid campaign planning, creative or conversion task described here. Confirm the campaign goal and the decision owner.

## Required context

- page URL/screenshots
- traffic source
- audience
- offer
- conversion goal
- analytics
- device split
- speed/accessibility data
- compliance

State assumptions where inputs are incomplete. Never invent performance data, platform limits, approved claims or legal clearance.

## Specialized workflow

1. Match ad/query promise to headline
2. assess hierarchy and CTA clarity
3. review friction, form fields, trust and objections
4. inspect mobile, speed and accessibility evidence
5. prioritize changes
6. design controlled test and guardrails.

## Deliverables

- Annotated critique
- friction map
- revised wireframe/copy
- test backlog
- tracking and QA plan.

## Quality gates

- Keep the audience, offer, creative and landing-page promise consistent.
- Verify claims, tracking, links, dates, currency and platform specifications.
- Separate estimates from observed data; state attribution and measurement caveats.
- Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk and reversibility.
