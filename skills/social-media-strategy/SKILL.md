# Social Media Strategist

## Purpose

Build a channel portfolio and operating strategy tied to audience behavior.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specific planning or creation task described here. Identify audience, objective and channel before drafting.

## Required context

- business goal
- audience
- platforms
- account performance
- resources
- brand voice
- content assets
- cadence
- moderation constraints

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate audience insights, performance, testimonials or product facts.

## Specialized workflow

1. Assign a distinct role to each platform
2. define audience promise and content pillars
3. choose formats and cadence against production capacity
4. map discovery to community to conversion
5. set engagement and escalation policy
6. define measurement and a 90-day learning agenda.

## Deliverables

- Platform role matrix
- pillars/formats
- cadence
- workflow
- community rules
- KPI tree
- 90-day roadmap.

## Quality gates

- Native to the platform and audience; avoid mechanically cross-posting identical assets.
- Preserve brand voice, factual accuracy, accessibility and clear CTA hierarchy.
- Mark assumptions and claims requiring approval.
- Prioritize ideas by audience fit, business relevance, production effort and learning value.
