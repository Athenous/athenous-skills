# Community Engagement Planner

## Purpose

Build a responsive community program with useful conversations and escalation.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specialized channel, content or community task described here. Clarify the intended audience and business outcome.

## Required context

- community purpose
- platforms
- audience
- moderation rules
- staffing
- response SLA
- FAQs
- brand boundaries

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate source material, customer quotes, platform results or product claims.

## Specialized workflow

1. Define community outcomes and norms
2. classify conversation types
3. create response principles and escalation paths
4. design recurring prompts and events
5. set moderation/crisis triggers
6. create staffing rota and templates
7. measure resolution and participation quality.

## Deliverables

- Community playbook
- engagement calendar
- response library
- escalation matrix
- staffing/SLA
- health metrics.

## Quality gates

- Preserve source meaning and brand voice; distinguish original material from interpretation.
- Respect platform format, accessibility, privacy, disclosure and content-rights requirements.
- Make each asset useful to its intended audience, not merely a shortened duplicate.
- Identify claims, examples or quotes requiring verification.

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm the audience, business objective, scope and decision this work should enable.
2. Review supplied evidence; check source, freshness, coverage, definitions and constraints.
3. Execute the specialized workflow above in sequence and show the reasoning behind material choices.
4. Rank outputs by business value, confidence, effort and risk; identify trade-offs and dependencies.
5. Provide a practical handoff: owner, next step, timing and validation measure. Clearly label assumptions and unknowns.

## Athenous MCP usage

## Approval and safety

Before replying as a brand, moderating/removing content, scheduling or publishing, present the exact action and obtain explicit confirmation. Escalate threats, personal data, legal issues and safety concerns to a responsible human. Never expose credentials or claim an action succeeded without tool confirmation.
