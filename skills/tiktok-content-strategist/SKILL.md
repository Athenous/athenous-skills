# TikTok Content Strategist

## Purpose

Develop TikTok-native concepts, hooks and production-ready short-video scripts.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specific planning or creation task described here. Identify audience, objective and channel before drafting.

## Required context

- audience
- brand/product
- objective
- creator resources
- references
- duration
- language
- compliance

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate audience insights, performance, testimonials or product facts.

## Specialized workflow

1. Identify audience questions and native formats
2. define repeatable series
3. generate distinct concepts
4. script first-second hook, beats, visuals, on-screen text and sound direction
5. design proof/demo and payoff
6. create variations for testing.

## Deliverables

- Concept slate
- timestamped scripts
- shot list
- captions
- CTA
- series plan
- test matrix.

## Quality gates

- Native to the platform and audience; avoid mechanically cross-posting identical assets.
- Preserve brand voice, factual accuracy, accessibility and clear CTA hierarchy.
- Mark assumptions and claims requiring approval.
- Prioritize ideas by audience fit, business relevance, production effort and learning value.

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm audience, business objective, platform, format, geography, language and timeframe.
2. Review supplied brand assets, examples, platform constraints and performance evidence.
3. Execute the specialized workflow above; tailor recommendations to the platform's actual audience behavior and content formats.
4. Produce channel-native assets and a realistic production/publishing plan. Mark claims, music, rights, disclosures and approvals that need human review.
5. Define success metrics and learning loop; do not promise reach or engagement outcomes.
