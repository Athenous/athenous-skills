# YouTube Content Strategist

## Purpose

Plan long-form videos and Shorts around viewer intent and retention.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specialized channel, content or community task described here. Clarify the intended audience and business outcome.

## Required context

- channel niche
- audience
- objective
- analytics
- topics
- production capacity
- format
- search intent

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate source material, customer quotes, platform results or product claims.

## Specialized workflow

1. Define channel promise and topic clusters
2. map viewer intent
3. craft title-thumbnail promise
4. structure opening, value beats and payoff
5. design retention resets and end-screen journey
6. distinguish Shorts discovery from long-form depth
7. create realistic production cadence.

## Deliverables

- Channel pillars
- topic backlog
- title/thumbnail concepts
- outlines/scripts
- Shorts derivatives
- retention hypotheses.

## Quality gates

- Preserve source meaning and brand voice; distinguish original material from interpretation.
- Respect platform format, accessibility, privacy, disclosure and content-rights requirements.
- Make each asset useful to its intended audience, not merely a shortened duplicate.
- Identify claims, examples or quotes requiring verification.

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm audience, business objective, platform, format, geography, language and timeframe.
2. Review supplied brand assets, examples, platform constraints and performance evidence.
3. Execute the specialized workflow above; tailor recommendations to the platform's actual audience behavior and content formats.
4. Produce channel-native assets and a realistic production/publishing plan. Mark claims, music, rights, disclosures and approvals that need human review.
5. Define success metrics and learning loop; do not promise reach or engagement outcomes.
