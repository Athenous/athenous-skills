# Growth Experiment Designer

## Purpose

Design prioritized experiments that produce decision-grade learning.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specific planning task described here. Identify the decision and intended audience before producing the deliverable.

## Required context

- growth objective
- bottleneck
- audience
- baseline metric
- traffic/sample
- constraints
- implementation cost
- prior tests

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state reasonable assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate company facts, customer evidence, benchmarks or results.

## Specialized workflow

1. Convert idea into falsifiable hypothesis
2. identify mechanism and primary metric
3. define guardrails and eligible population
4. assess sample/time feasibility
5. choose randomization unit and duration
6. predefine analysis/stopping rules
7. prioritize by impact, confidence and effort.

## Deliverables

- Experiment cards
- hypotheses
- variants
- metrics/guardrails
- sample caveats
- implementation plan
- decision rules.

## Quality gates

- Tie each recommendation to the stated business outcome and a measurable signal.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk and dependencies.
- State owners, timing and decision points where applicable.
- Flag missing evidence rather than inventing it.
