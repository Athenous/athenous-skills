# SEO Strategy Planner

## Purpose

Create a sustainable search strategy aligned with audience demand and business value.

## Use this Skill when

Use for measurement, organic search or performance investigation as described. Establish scope, source coverage and the decision the analysis should support.

## Required context

- site/business
- market/language
- products
- audience
- query research
- Search Console
- competitors
- resources
- conversion goals

State limitations where data is missing. Never fabricate search volumes, rankings, conversions, statistical significance or root causes.

## Specialized workflow

1. Map topics to intent and funnel
2. prioritize clusters by relevance and attainable opportunity
3. define pillar/supporting architecture
4. plan technical foundations and internal links
5. align briefs to expertise and proof
6. plan ethical authority building
7. define measurement and refresh cycles.

## Deliverables

- SEO roadmap
- topic clusters
- page priorities
- content briefs
- technical backlog
- authority plan
- KPIs.

## Quality gates

- Preserve metric definitions, time windows and source provenance.
- Distinguish observed facts, modeled estimates, interpretations and causal claims.
- Prioritize work by business relevance, confidence, effort and risk.
- Avoid manipulative SEO, scaled low-value content and unsupported ranking guarantees.
- Identify data coverage and instrumentation limitations.



## Ready-to-use workflow

### Copy/paste prompt

```text
Apply seo-strategist to [website/brand/client]. Objective: [business outcome]. Audience/market: [details]. Period/scope: [dates and channels]. Inputs: [analytics, exports, URLs, content]. Constraints: [brand, legal, budget]. Check: site/business; market/language; products; audience; query research; Search Console; competitors; resources; conversion goals. Validate evidence and definitions; ask one critical question only if needed. Return complete deliverables with transparent assumptions and actionable priorities.
```

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm site/brand/client, target audience, objective, scope, date range and desired decision.
2. Inventory sources and validate freshness, coverage, definitions, language/market and any sampling limitations.
3. Apply the specialized workflow above. Support claims with supplied data or linked evidence; separate fact from inference.
4. Prioritize actions by business relevance, confidence, effort and risk; state dependencies and what to test next.
5. Deliver implementation-ready recommendations or copy, plus measurement and review criteria.

### Output contract

Lead with the most important conclusion. Include:

- **SEO roadmap:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **topic clusters:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **page priorities:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **content briefs:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **technical backlog:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **authority plan:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.
- **KPIs.:** concrete finding or asset, evidence and next action.

Also state scope, source links/data coverage, assumptions, limitations, and a prioritized next-step list. Do not invent search volume, rankings, sentiment, reach, revenue or performance. Mark unknowns **Not provided**.

## Athenous MCP usage

MCP is optional. Discover exact available analytics, reporting or content tools at runtime; never invent tool names, fields or capabilities. Use only authorized data and keep client workspaces isolated. If SEO/search data is not available through MCP, use supplied exports or clearly labeled public evidence.

## Safety

Protect personal and customer data. Do not change live pages, metadata, budgets or campaigns without presenting the exact change and receiving explicit confirmation. Never claim a change or test outcome without tool confirmation.
