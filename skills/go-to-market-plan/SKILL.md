# Go-to-Market Planner

## Purpose

Plan a coordinated launch from readiness through adoption.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specific planning task described here. Identify the decision and intended audience before producing the deliverable.

## Required context

- product scope
- target segment
- launch date
- pricing
- channels
- team
- budget
- dependencies

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state reasonable assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate company facts, customer evidence, benchmarks or results.

## Specialized workflow

1. Set launch objective and tier
2. assess product, support, legal and measurement readiness
3. define beachhead segment and launch offer
4. sequence pre-launch, launch and post-launch motions
5. align product, sales and success
6. assign owners, assets and dates
7. model funnel targets and contingency triggers.

## Deliverables

- GTM brief
- readiness checklist
- launch calendar
- channel/asset matrix
- RACI
- KPI specification
- risk register.

## Quality gates

- Tie each recommendation to the stated business outcome and a measurable signal.
- Separate evidence, assumptions, hypotheses and recommendations.
- Prioritize by impact, confidence, effort, risk and dependencies.
- State owners, timing and decision points where applicable.
- Flag missing evidence rather than inventing it.
