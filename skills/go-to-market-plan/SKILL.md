# Go-to-Market Planner

## Purpose

Plan a coordinated launch from readiness through adoption.

## Use this Skill when

The user needs the specialized workflow below. First identify the decision or deliverable they need; do not force this workflow onto unrelated requests.

## Required context

- product and launch scope
- target segment
- value proposition
- launch date
- pricing
- channels
- team
- budget
- dependencies
- success targets

Ask only for the most important missing input. If a reasonable assumption allows progress, state it and continue. Never invent data, customer evidence, platform capabilities, benchmarks, testimonials, or results.

## Workflow

1. Set launch objective and tier
2. assess product, support, legal and measurement readiness
3. define beachhead segment and launch offer
4. map pre-launch, launch and post-launch motions
5. align product, sales, success and marketing
6. create channel sequencing, asset list and owners
7. model funnel targets and contingency triggers.

## Deliverables

- GTM brief
- readiness checklist
- audience and offer
- launch calendar
- channel/asset matrix
- owner RACI
- KPI dashboard specification
- risk register.

## Quality and decision rules

- Separate committed capabilities from planned features
- do not announce unverified integrations or dates
- include rollback/contingency.
- State the period, currency, scope, definitions and assumptions wherever they affect interpretation.
- Separate observed facts, calculations, interpretations and recommendations.
- Rank actions by expected impact, confidence, effort, risk and dependencies; explain the trade-off.
- If evidence is incomplete, identify the missing evidence and provide a useful provisional answer without disguising uncertainty.
