# Client Strategy Presentation Planner

## Purpose

Structure a persuasive, evidence-led strategy presentation for client decisions.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the agency operations or growth problem described here. Clarify whether the deliverable is a process, analysis, recommendation or client-facing artifact.

## Required context

- client context
- objective
- audience
- research
- performance data
- strategic choices
- budget
- scope
- desired decision

Ask only for critical missing inputs; state assumptions otherwise. Do not invent financial results, staffing commitments, approvals or process steps.

## Specialized workflow

1. Define the decision the meeting must produce
2. build narrative from context to insight to choice
3. prioritize evidence and implications
4. show alternatives and trade-offs
5. translate strategy into roadmap, investment and ownership
6. prepare objections and appendix
7. end with explicit approvals.

## Deliverables

- Slide storyline
- headline-led titles
- evidence per slide
- speaker notes
- visual suggestions
- decision slide.

## Quality gates

- Make recommendations executable by a named role with clear timing and acceptance criteria.
- Account for margin, workload, risk and client experience, not just volume or speed.
- Separate documented facts from assumptions and proposed policy.
- Include escalation, review and feedback loops where the process is recurring.



## Ready-to-use workflow

```text
Apply client-strategy-presentation to [business/client]. Desired outcome: [goal]. Audience: [who will use the result]. Period/scope: [dates]. Context and constraints: [brief]. Evidence: [paste exports, URLs, examples]. Check: client context; objective; audience; research; performance data; strategic choices; budget; scope; desired decision. Ask only one blocking question if necessary; otherwise state assumptions and complete the deliverables. Cite source data where possible and do not invent results.
```

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm the audience, business objective, scope and decision this work should enable.
2. Review supplied evidence; check source, freshness, coverage, definitions and constraints.
3. Execute the specialized workflow above in sequence and show the reasoning behind material choices.
4. Rank outputs by business value, confidence, effort and risk; identify trade-offs and dependencies.
5. Provide a practical handoff: owner, next step, timing and validation measure. Clearly label assumptions and unknowns.

### Output contract

Start with a concise recommendation, followed by:

- **Slide storyline:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **headline-led titles:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **evidence per slide:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **speaker notes:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **visual suggestions:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **decision slide.:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.

Include scope, sources, assumptions, limitations and next steps. Use structured tables where they improve actionability. Mark missing information **Not provided**; do not fabricate metrics, customer statements or outcomes.
