# Content Calendar Planner

## Purpose

Create a realistic editorial calendar with channel-native assets and owners.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the specific planning or creation task described here. Identify audience, objective and channel before drafting.

## Required context

- campaign dates
- audience
- channels
- content pillars
- launches
- assets
- cadence
- owners
- approval lead times

Ask only for a critical missing input; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Never fabricate audience insights, performance, testimonials or product facts.

## Specialized workflow

1. Map business moments and audience needs
2. assign purpose and funnel stage to each item
3. balance pillars, formats and themes
4. schedule production and approvals backwards from publish date
5. link repurposing
6. detect workload conflicts.

## Deliverables

- Calendar with date, channel, format, pillar, hook, CTA, asset, owner, status and KPI
- production checklist.

## Quality gates

- Native to the platform and audience; avoid mechanically cross-posting identical assets.
- Preserve brand voice, factual accuracy, accessibility and clear CTA hierarchy.
- Mark assumptions and claims requiring approval.
- Prioritize ideas by audience fit, business relevance, production effort and learning value.


### Execution checklist

1. Confirm the audience, business objective, scope and decision this work should enable.
2. Review supplied evidence; check source, freshness, coverage, definitions and constraints.
3. Execute the specialized workflow above in sequence and show the reasoning behind material choices.
4. Rank outputs by business value, confidence, effort and risk; identify trade-offs and dependencies.
5. Provide a practical handoff: owner, next step, timing and validation measure. Clearly label assumptions and unknowns.

### Output contract

Start with a concise recommendation, followed by:

- **Calendar with date, channel, format, pillar, hook, CTA, asset, owner, status and KPI:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.
- **production checklist.:** include a usable artifact, rationale and next action.

Include scope, sources, assumptions, limitations and next steps. Use structured tables where they improve actionability. Mark missing information **Not provided**; do not fabricate metrics, customer statements or outcomes.
