# Agency Client Monthly Report

## Purpose

Create a transparent client review that connects performance to decisions.

## Use this Skill when

Use to design or operate agency client delivery. Establish the client, service scope and accountable owners before making recommendations.

## Required context

- client goals
- period
- approved metrics
- platform/CRM data
- prior month
- budget
- activities
- context
- next-month priorities

Ask for critical missing authorization or scope details; otherwise state assumptions. Never assume access, approval or a contractual commitment.

## Specialized workflow

1. Confirm scope and reconcile sources
2. compare outcomes to target and prior period
3. explain drivers with evidence
4. report deliverables and spend transparently
5. distinguish platform attribution from business results
6. surface risks and learning
7. agree next-month decisions and owners.

## Deliverables

- Client narrative
- KPI scorecard
- channel summaries
- work completed
- insights
- recommendations
- approvals
- next-month plan.

## Quality gates

- Keep each client's assets, credentials, reporting and approvals isolated.
- Use least-privilege access; never request or store passwords or tokens.
- Make ownership, scope, approval status and handoffs explicit.
- Include exceptions, offboarding and auditability where relevant.



## Ready-to-use workflow

### Copy/paste prompt

```text
Use client-monthly-report as a senior specialist for [business/client]. Goal: [specific outcome]. Period/scope: [dates and scope]. Context: [business, audience, offer]. Inputs: [paste data/files/URLs]. Constraints: [budget, resources, brand, permissions]. Check these inputs: client goals; period; approved metrics; platform/CRM data; prior month; budget; activities; context; next-month priorities. Ask only for the most blocking missing detail; otherwise state assumptions and proceed. Deliver the full output contract. Separate evidence, calculations, assumptions and recommendations. Do not execute live changes.
```

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm client/business, objective, decision, scope and date range.
2. Inventory available evidence and validate source, freshness, units, definitions and coverage.
3. Apply the specialized workflow above; show calculations and evidence for material conclusions.
4. Rank recommendations by impact, confidence, effort, risk and dependencies.
5. Provide owners, next steps and validation criteria. Label unknowns; never fill data gaps with invented values.

### Output contract

Lead with the answer and decision required. Include:

- **Client narrative:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **KPI scorecard:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **channel summaries:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **work completed:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **insights:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **recommendations:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **approvals:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.
- **next-month plan.:** decision-ready content, evidence and next action.

Also state scope, period, sources, data coverage, assumptions, limitations and the three highest-priority next actions. Use tables for comparisons. Use **Not provided** for unavailable values.

## Athenous MCP usage

MCP is optional. Discover actual workspace, client, connection and workflow tools and schemas at runtime. Never assume a feature or permission exists. ## Approval and safety

Never infer client consent from silence or agency access. Before inviting users, changing workspace access, approving or launching campaigns, changing spend or automating external actions, show the exact operation and obtain explicit confirmation from the authorized person. Follow stricter server-side rules. Never expose secrets or claim completion without tool confirmation.
