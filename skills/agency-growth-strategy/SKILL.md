# Marketing Agency Growth Strategist

## Purpose

Build an agency growth plan around niche, positioning, pipeline and delivery economics.

## Use this Skill when

Use for the agency operations or growth problem described here. Clarify whether the deliverable is a process, analysis, recommendation or client-facing artifact.

## Required context

- agency size
- services
- niche
- revenue/margins
- client concentration
- pipeline
- conversion
- capacity
- churn
- founder goals

Ask only for critical missing inputs; state assumptions otherwise. Do not invent financial results, staffing commitments, approvals or process steps.

## Specialized workflow

1. Diagnose growth constraint
2. assess niche and service profitability
3. sharpen positioning and packaged offers
4. map referral, outbound, partner and content channels
5. build qualification/sales process
6. connect pipeline to delivery capacity
7. model retention, expansion and hiring
8. sequence 90-day experiments.

## Deliverables

- Growth diagnosis
- ICP/niche recommendation
- offer architecture
- pipeline math
- channel plan
- capacity guardrails
- 90-day roadmap.

## Quality gates

- Make recommendations executable by a named role with clear timing and acceptance criteria.
- Account for margin, workload, risk and client experience, not just volume or speed.
- Separate documented facts from assumptions and proposed policy.
- Include escalation, review and feedback loops where the process is recurring.



## Ready-to-use workflow

### Copy/paste prompt

```text
Use agency-growth-strategy for [agency]. Goal: [revenue, margin, client or capacity target]. Current baseline: [clients, services, team, revenue/margin if supplied]. ICP/market: [segments/geography]. Time horizon: [period]. Constraints: [budget, team, delivery capacity]. Inputs: [data]. Check: agency size; services; niche; revenue/margins; client concentration; pipeline; conversion; capacity; churn; founder goals. Ask one critical question only if blocked; otherwise state assumptions and deliver a prioritized growth plan.
```

### Execution checklist

1. Confirm agency model, service mix, target client profile, objective, baseline and time horizon.
2. Validate evidence on pipeline, conversion, retention, pricing, margin and delivery capacity; flag missing data.
3. Apply the specialized workflow above and model trade-offs between acquisition, expansion, retention and operational capacity.
4. Rank initiatives by expected value, confidence, effort, risk and time-to-signal.
5. Translate the strategy into owners, milestones, leading indicators and review cadence.

### Output contract

Lead with the growth thesis and key decision. Include:

- **Growth diagnosis:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **ICP/niche recommendation:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **offer architecture:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **pipeline math:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **channel plan:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **capacity guardrails:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.
- **90-day roadmap.:** concrete recommendation, owner and next action.

Also include baseline and assumptions, risks, dependencies, what not to pursue, and a 30/60/90-day action plan. Mark unknown values **Not provided**; do not fabricate revenue, margins or conversion rates.
